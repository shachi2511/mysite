Schedulr

it builds your week so you don't have to.

  • You give it your tasks and fixed commitments, and it works out when everything should happen
  • A greedy scheduling algorithm drops each task into your first free block before its deadline, and tells you honestly when something won't fit
  • React and TypeScript on the front end, FastAPI and MongoDB on the back, deployed on AWS
ReactTypeScriptFastAPIMongoDBAWS

Decode

paste dense technical docs, get them back in plain English.

  • Explains documentation, error messages, or specs that assume you already know the jargon
  • Full RAG pipeline: chunks and embeds text with SentenceTransformers, retrieves from ChromaDB, generates with Claude
  • Works on raw text or whole PDFs
PythonFastAPIChromaDBClaude API

Hotel Management System

ShrinX

drop in a PDF, get a whole study kit back.

  • Turns any PDF into seven study formats: summary, notes, flashcards, MCQs, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, Q&A
  • Flask app that parses the PDF and calls Claude to generate each format

Clinical Quality & Patient Outcomes Analytics

does a hospital's star rating actually mean better care? the data says: barely.

  • Analyzes CMS data on 5,000+ U.S. hospitals to see what actually predicts patient outcomes
  • Built a full pipeline: clean the data, model it in SQL, then visualize it in an interactive dashboard
  • Turns out star ratings barely predict readmission rates — they explain under 19% of the variance
PythonpandasSciPySQLSQLiteJavaScript

Emotion & Empathy Prediction

teaching a model to read the room in a conversation — then keep it going.

  • Predicts how emotional and empathetic each turn of a conversation is
  • Compared three models — a simple network, an LSTM, and BERT — to see which read tone best (BERT won)
  • Also generates realistic next turns in a conversation based on that emotional context

About Me

By Shachi Shriwastava · Staff Writer

Hey, I’m Shachi! I recently graduated from UIC with a bachelor’s in computer science, and I’m really into AI right now. I talk to AI more than I’d like to admit (it still doesn’t remember me, rude). I like building stuff that’s actually useful, checking if it really is, and sometimes writing about what I figure out.

This site is where I’ve collected some of the things I’ve done, and since I’m a little obsessed with word puzzles, I made a few of my own. You’ll find them waiting at the end.
